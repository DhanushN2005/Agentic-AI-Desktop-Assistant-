Say it, and your PC does it.

Flexie OS is an assistant that runs on your Windows computer. Tell it by voice or text what you want: it opens apps, changes settings, reads your screen, works in your browser, builds small apps, and remembers how you like things done.

What Flexie can do

You don't need special commands. Talk the way you would to a person, and Flexie works out what you mean, including what you mean by "that".

The lines in blue are real things you can say. Flexie runs on Windows.

Run your PC

Volume, brightness, apps, files and folders, timers, alarms and reminders, battery and Wi-Fi status. When more than one Chrome window is open, it asks which one you mean.

  • increase volume
  • brightness 40
  • take a screenshot
  • remind me in 5 minutes to stretch

See your screen

Flexie can look at what is on your screen and explain it in plain words, like an error message or an unfamiliar page.

  • summarize my screen
  • what's wrong on my screen
  • read my screen

Work in your browser

With its Chrome extension, Flexie can read, summarize and pull tables from the page you are on. Anything that changes the page, such as a click or typing, waits for your approval first.

  • summarize this page
  • extract tables from this page
  • click sign in on this page

Build small apps

Describe an app and Flexie plans it, creates the files, runs the tests and checks, and launches it for you. You can watch each phase on the dashboard.

  • build me a to-do app
  • open the project

Understand what you mean

Flexie keeps track of what it just made or showed, so "that" and "it" make sense. If two things are equally likely, it asks. You can ask it to repeat, shorten or undo.

  • open that screenshot
  • say that again
  • make it shorter
  • undo that

Remember you

Tell Flexie how you like things and it keeps them, then uses them in its answers. You can see and delete everything it remembers in Settings. It never keeps passwords, keys or card numbers.

  • I prefer short answers
  • call me Sam
  • what are my preferences

Pick your AI

Use Groq, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek or Mistral, or a model that runs on your own computer with Ollama. Flexie falls back to the next one if a provider fails, and you can switch in Settings without restarting.

Built to be careful

An assistant that can control your computer has to earn trust. These are the rules Flexie follows.

It asks first

Sending mail, deleting files and changing a web page all wait for your approval.

Your keys stay home

API keys, memory and preferences are stored on your PC, not on a server.

Local only

The dashboard only accepts connections from the computer it runs on, with a private token for each session.

Web text can't give orders

What Flexie reads on a page is treated as information, never as instructions.

A dashboard for it all

See your computer's health, what Flexie has been doing, which AI providers it uses and what they cost, and change how it behaves, all in one place.

Who built this

Flexie OS is a project by Dhanush N, who builds LLM-powered tools, from search over documents to voice assistants to 3D learning aids.

The idea was an assistant that does things on your computer instead of only talking about them, and that stays honest about what it did and what it can't do. It plans multi-step tasks, checks its own work, and keeps your data on your machine.
