description: Search the web and produce a structured, cited summary
tags: [research, summarization]
---

When asked to research a topic:

1. Search at least three sources.
2. Prefer primary sources and recent material.
3. Extract claims, not marketing copy.
4. Return: a five-sentence summary, three key facts, and a source list with URLs.
5. If sources disagree, say so explicitly.
